In this experiment we examine all network interfaces traffic on ports 80 and 53. Port 80 and 53 are dedicated for HTTP traffic and DNS traffic, repectively. HTTP protocol is for connection between web servers and web browsers. DNS protocol is for converting Domain address ganted by the client to its IP address.  In the traffic at port 80, I have seen the dominance of TCP protocol as a result of its role in HTTP connection. There were a lot of TCP messages for 3-way handshake and some HTTP messages. for port 53, there were just DNS protocol messages, most of which are successful and others not.
